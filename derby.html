Home Run Derby

This version uses a 3×3 strike zone. Move the yellow batting cursor with WASD/arrows or tap the zone, then swing with Space. You need the right location + timing. Every 5th pitch is a gold bonus ball worth 2 HR.

WASD/arrows = move batting cursor · Space = swing · gold ball = 2 HR
